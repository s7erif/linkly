# Linkly

<p align="center">
  <strong>Open-source digital business cards powered by NFC, QR codes, and AI.</strong>
</p>

## 🌍 Overview

Linkly is an open-source platform that enables individuals and businesses to create reusable digital business cards. Instead of printing paper or plastic cards, users can instantly share their professional profile using NFC technology or QR codes.

Our mission is to modernize networking while reducing waste from traditional business cards, making professional connections faster, smarter, and more environmentally friendly.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📱 NFC-powered digital business cards
- 🔗 QR code sharing
- 👤 Custom public profile pages
- 📇 Contact information management
- 🌐 Social media and website links
- 🎨 Modern responsive UI
- 🔒 Secure authentication
- ⚡ Fast and lightweight experience
- 🤖 AI-powered profile enhancements (coming soon)

---

## 🌱 Sustainability

Every year, millions of paper and plastic business cards are produced, used briefly, and discarded.

Linkly helps reduce this waste by replacing disposable physical cards with reusable digital profiles that can be updated anytime without reprinting.

Our goal is to encourage a more sustainable and environmentally conscious way of networking.
